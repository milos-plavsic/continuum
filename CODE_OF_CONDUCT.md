# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We as members, contributors, and leaders pledge to make participation in our community a
harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible
disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of
experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race,
caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming, diverse,
inclusive, and healthy community.

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment include empathy, kindness,
respectful disagreement, constructive feedback, accepting responsibility, and focusing on
the health of the community. Unacceptable behavior includes sexualized conduct, trolling,
insults, personal or political attacks, harassment, publishing private information without
permission, or other conduct reasonably considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement Responsibilities

Project leaders must clarify and fairly enforce these standards. They may remove, edit, or
reject comments, commits, code, issues, and other contributions that violate them, and will
communicate reasons when appropriate.

## Scope

This Code applies in project spaces and when a person officially represents the project in
public, including hackathon, social-media, and collaboration channels.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behavior privately to `milos.plavsic@googlemail.com` with subject
`[Continuum conduct]`. Reports will be reviewed promptly and fairly, with the reporter's
privacy and security protected. If a report concerns the maintainer, use the host platform's
trust-and-safety reporting channel rather than sending it to the subject of the report.

Leaders apply the Contributor Covenant 2.1 community-impact ladder: correction, warning,
temporary ban, or permanent ban, proportionate to impact and pattern. Retaliation against a
good-faith reporter is itself a violation.
