Target duration: 3:42–3:50 including deliberate pauses. Voice: Orus, English,
using Gemini 3.1 Flash TTS Preview, followed by the locked 1.15x
pitch-preserving tempo pass. Each numbered scene is generated as an independent
audio clip.

The narration describes significance while the screen supplies identifiers and
raw proof. Do not make the voice read hashes, revision names, or service-account
addresses.

## Claims that must remain scoped

- Say “one effect under the demonstrated gateway/provider failure model,” not
  universal exactly-once execution.
- Say “reference binding” and “portable contract,” not cloud-independent proof
  for the deployed application.
- Do not claim Byzantine fault tolerance, global revocation, third-party
  interoperability, or model determinism.
- Gemini selects only from the deterministically eligible set; policy remains
  authoritative.
- Describe Gemini, Gemma, Veo, and Lyria collectively as four Google generative
  models. Veo and Lyria are not LLMs.
- The public multimodal receipt is a separately released verified proof. Never
  place its attestation or media identifiers under the fresh run's `SAME-RUN
  PROOF` label.
