# Continuum submission video — proof-first production

Status: production started August 28, 2026  
Working title: **Continuum — The Promise Survives**  
Target runtime: **3:54** (hard ceiling 3:58)  
Category: **Fortified Enterprise Fleet**

## Creative decision

This is a new film, not a revision of the 3:59.88 remarkable master. The old
master remains an untouched fallback. The new production uses the accepted v19
cloud capture and exact-release evidence as source material, but replaces the
opening, narration, timing, editorial grammar, and claim order.

The film behaves like a short cross-examination. Each act asks one operational
question and immediately shows the native evidence that answers it:

1. Can Continuum detect a failure that emits no error?
2. Who is eligible to inherit the obligation?
3. What crosses the handoff boundary?
4. Does the successor complete useful work?
5. Can redelivery duplicate the external effect?
6. Who is allowed to declare success?

## Why this replaces the previous approach

The official pre-submission guidance says to show the working project in the
first 10–15 seconds, keep the film under four minutes, use one strong example,
and remove dead time. The earlier master spends three seconds on a creator card,
runs to 3:59.88, and allocates too much of its final act to architecture and the
bonus-model chain. It is technically dense but gives a tired judge too many
things to retain.

This cut starts on the live Cloud Run cockpit at frame one. The single start
click occurs at approximately 5.2 seconds. The product thesis is earned through
the handoff and verifier, not announced on a title card.

## Non-negotiable evidence rules

- Preserve the untouched canonical screen recording and its manifest.
- Show the `.run.app` control surface at 0:04 and the single start click by 0:09.
- Label every temporal cut `SAME RUN · TRANSPARENT TIME CUT` for at least 0.8 s.
- Never splice observations from another lifecycle under a same-run label.
- Do not repair or replace native runtime values with invented UI.
- Editorial zooms and question straps may guide attention but may not cover a
  contradictory value. Frames containing the obsolete v18 handoff label are
  excluded rather than corrected.
- Bind exact-release proof to commit `d4d7d52`, run
  `judge-final-d4d7d52-20260827T223700Z`, 17 objects, 174 spans, and offline
  `PASS`.
- Describe the live GitHub work item as a reversible synthetic effect and scope
  idempotency to the demonstrated gateway and reconcilable provider.
- Describe the Continuity Contract as a proposal and portable boundary, not an
  adopted standard.
- Keep Gemma, Veo, and Lyria downstream of independent verification and label
  their outputs derivative, never authority or evidence.

## Visual language

- Native product fills the frame; no decorative frame, logo animation, or fake
  terminal.
- A restrained upper-right question strap supplies the chapter question.
- A lower-left evidence slug identifies `CONTINUOUS LIVE CAPTURE`, `SAME-RUN
  EVIDENCE`, `EXACT-RELEASE CLOUD PROOF`, or `REFERENCE ARCHITECTURE`.
- Cuts are hard and honest. Architecture gets 12 seconds; the bonus-model chain
  gets 8 seconds; the end card gets 10 seconds.
- No background music under the click, predecessor denial, one-effect metric,
  or verifier verdict. If music is retained at all, use only the verifier-gated
  Lyria cue at very low level during chapter transitions and the close.

## Voice direction

Preferred final voice is the creator's own voice: calm, direct, and recorded in
short scene takes. This improves authenticity and avoids the polished synthetic
trailer quality of the earlier master. A generated voice remains a fallback,
not the default.

The voice explains why the evidence matters. It never reads hashes, service
account names, or every technology logo. On-screen text carries those facts.

## Acceptance gate

The new master is accepted only if all answers below are yes:

- Does the working product appear by 0:04 and the start click occur by 0:09?
- Is the runtime at most 3:58.00 with upload/transcode margin below the four-minute rule?
- Can a viewer state the product in one sentence after one viewing?
- Is Gemini visibly useful but never authoritative?
- Are the predecessor denial, 2-in/4-out context boundary, one effect, and
  verifier-only verdict legible at normal 1080p playback?
- Is every same-run or exact-release number traceable to committed evidence?

- Are captions present and manually checked?
