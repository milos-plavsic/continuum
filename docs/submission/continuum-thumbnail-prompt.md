# Continuum submission-thumbnail generation prompt

Use case: ads-marketing

Asset type: Devpost hackathon project thumbnail and LinkedIn launch image

Primary request: Create a premium, technically credible hero image for an
enterprise agent-continuity product called CONTINUUM.

Scene/backdrop: deep midnight-navy field with a restrained architectural grid,
subtle cloud-system traces, and cinematic teal light; refined and uncluttered.

Subject: a single glowing amber "promise" represented as a small luminous
capsule travels across a fortified continuity bridge from a dim, fractured
crimson predecessor-agent node on the left to a stable emerald successor-agent
node on the right. Around the handoff, show a clean ring of six cryptographic
evidence artifacts; the final artifact carries a clear verified check. The
visual story must read instantly as: the agent can fail, but its obligation
survives, is verified, and continues exactly once.

Style/medium: state-of-the-art enterprise technology editorial illustration;
polished cinematic 3D combined with precise data-visualization geometry;
sophisticated, credible, memorable; not generic AI art.

Composition/framing: 3:2 landscape, optimized for a thumbnail at small size.
One strong central diagonal flow and obvious visual hierarchy. Leave calm
negative space in the upper-left for title copy. Keep all key elements safely
inside the central 80% for platform cropping.

Lighting/mood: confident, resilient, secure; subtle volumetric glow, crisp
edges, premium contrast.

Color palette: midnight navy, electric cyan and teal, amber promise light,
emerald verification, very limited crimson only for failed predecessor.

Text (verbatim): "CONTINUUM" on one line, then "THE AGENT FAILED. THE PROMISE
DID NOT." beneath it. Render exactly once in clean bold geometric sans-serif
typography, highly legible, no spelling changes.

Constraints: no humanoid robots, no people, no chat bubbles, no fake
application UI, no source-code text, no Google, LinkedIn, Devpost, or other
corporate logos, no additional words, no watermark. The handoff, six evidence
artifacts, and one verified outcome must remain visually clear even at thumbnail
size.
